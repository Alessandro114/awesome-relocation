## Cost of Living Data

- [**ReloMap**](https://relomap.app) — Free platform comparing 208 cities on cost of living, safety, climate, visas, and taxes. Updated monthly from Numbeo, Expatistan, and official sources. Includes tax calculator and AI-powered relocation snapshots.
- [Numbeo](https://www.numbeo.com) — Crowdsourced cost of living database. Largest dataset but prices can be outdated.
- [Expatistan](https://www.expatistan.com) — Cost of living comparisons using a basket of goods methodology.
- [Livingcost.org](https://livingcost.org) — Cost of living estimates with local purchasing power context.

## Visa & Immigration

- [**ReloMap Visa Guides**](https://relomap.app/blog) — In-depth visa guides for 208 destinations (digital nomad visas, residency permits, retirement visas).
- [VisaGuide.World](https://visaguide.world) — Visa requirements checker by nationality.
- [Nomad Visa Guide](https://nomadvisaguide.com) — Focused on digital nomad visa programs.
- [Immigration World](https://www.immigration-world.com) — Country-by-country immigration requirements.

## Tax Planning

- [**ReloMap Tax Calculator**](https://relomap.app/tax-calculator) — Compare tax burden between countries, including special regimes (Portugal NHR, Spain Beckham Law, UAE 0% income tax).
- [Tax Foundation](https://taxfoundation.org/data/all/global/) — International tax data and comparisons.
- [OECD Tax Database](https://www.oecd.org/tax/tax-policy/tax-database/) — Official tax rates by country.

## Health Insurance

- [SafetyWing](https://safetywing.com/?referenceID=26525115) — Nomad health insurance starting at $45/month. Global coverage.
- [Cigna Global](https://www.cigna.com/individuals-families/health-insurance/global/) — Premium international health insurance.
- [World Nomads](https://www.worldnomads.com/) — Travel insurance for short-term stays.

## Banking & Finance

- [Wise](https://wise.com/invite/dic/alessandrob1684) — Multi-currency account, works in 80+ countries. Best exchange rates.
- [Revolut](https://www.revolut.com/) — Digital banking with multi-currency support.
- [N26](https://n26.com/) — European digital bank, good for EU residents.
